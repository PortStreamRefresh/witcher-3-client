## 🧩 About

**The Witcher 3 Client** is a comprehensive overlay and trainer for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt featuring an in-game menu, infinite health, stamina, money editor, item spawner, quest tracker, and HUD customization.

Based on popular mods like **TW3 Trainer**, **Overlay Menu**, and **HUD Customizer**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Hacks
- Infinite Health — never die in combat
- Infinite Stamina — unlimited sprinting and signs
- Money Editor — set any amount of crowns
- Item Spawner — add any weapon, armor, or potion
- Teleport to Waypoint — fast travel from anywhere

### 🎨 Cosmetics & Unlocks
- Unlock All Gwent Cards — complete your collection
- Unlock All Mutations — access every skill
- Unlock All Armor Sets — witcher gear for free
- Hair and Beard Customization — change Geralt's look

### 🏋️ Practice & Training
- Quest Tracker — full quest log with map markers
- Enemy ESP — see enemies through walls
- Loot Radar — highlight nearby containers
- Infinite Oils — never run out of blade coatings
- Custom HUD — resize and move UI elements
